## What are face weight and pile height?

When you start comparing turf products, two numbers will show up again and again: pile height and total weight.

**Pile height** refers to how tall the individual grass blades stand. A taller pile height tends to look more plush and full, while a shorter pile height is easier to keep clean and provides a truer roll, which is why it is often used for [putting greens](/putting-green/).

**Total weight (face weight)** is measured in ounces per square yard and tells you how dense the turf actually is. The higher the ounce count, the more synthetic yarn is packed into the product, which means a thicker, more durable lawn that can take a beating.

Once you understand these two numbers, choosing the right turf becomes a lot less intimidating. To make it even easier, we have broken down our inventory into a simple "good, better, best" framework based on face weight and expected foot traffic.

### Good: 70–75oz for low traffic and aesthetic focus

If your goal is a front yard refresh or a landscaping accent area that people mostly look at rather than walk on, you do not need to look for extreme durability. Lighter face weights are built for aesthetic, low traffic zones, and they deliver a gorgeous, realistic look without the added cost of a heavier product.

- **[Bermuda Blend 70](/bermuda-blend/)**: A 70oz weight with a 1.5 inch pile height in an earthy olive and green tone that blends naturally into desert landscaping.
- **[Natural Blend 75](/natural-series/)**: A 75oz weight with a 1.5 inch pile height, designed to closely mimic the look of natural grass.

### Better: 84oz to 93oz for moderate traffic and versatility

For a standard backyard DIY installation, a mid range face weight strikes the right balance between cost and performance. These turfs are built for light to moderate foot traffic, making them a great fit for everyday family use.

- **[Winter Blend 84](/winter-series/)**: An 84oz weight with a 1.65 inch pile height in a cool toned green that stays vibrant under the Arizona sun.
- **[Spring Blend 87](/spring-blend/)**: An 87oz weight with a 1.65 inch pile height, offering a plush, vibrant look with strong curb appeal.
- **[Bermuda Blend 93](/bermuda-blend-93/)**: Steps the density up to 93oz with a taller 1.8 inch pile height for a fuller feel underfoot.

### Best: 103oz to 107oz for heavy foot traffic and pets

For backyards, play areas, and active dogs, you need a heavy duty solution. A higher total weight paired with a taller pile height gives you the thickest turf available, and that added density resists matting even under constant running, playing, and digging.

- **[Winter Blend 103](/winter-blend-103/)**: A 103oz weight with a 1.7 inch pile height, built for moderate to heavy traffic areas.
- **[Spring Blend 107](/spring-blend-107/)**: A massive 107oz weight with a 1.75 inch pile height, our top recommendation for heavy backyard use and busy pet families.
